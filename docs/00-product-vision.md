# 00 — Product vision and positioning

## Why Etfago exists
Real-life commitments are often agreed by calls, messages, quotes, or informal conversation. Later parties disagree about details: **which version was accepted, which changes were merely suggested, and what remains to be done**. Etfago creates a shared, structured, time-ordered home for those details.

## Jobs to be done
1. **Before commitment:** "Help me state the arrangement in understandable terms so every participant sees the same text."
2. **At commitment:** "Show exactly what each participant accepted, and who has not responded."
3. **During execution:** "When something changes, keep the current agreed terms intact until the new ones are accepted."
4. **Afterward:** "Give me a readable history of terms, decisions, discussions, and completion."

## Primary audience
- Individuals: shared expenses, group projects, responsibility splits, informal service arrangements.
- Service providers / clients: repairs, custom manufacturing, events, design, installations.
- Small businesses: supplier/client deliveries, scoped services, operational commitments.

No user category is forced into business-only fields. Participants can be individuals or organization representatives. The system does not verify organization signatory authority.

## Product principles
1. **Shared truth instead of one-sided notes.** An agreement becomes 'agreed' only after required active invited parties act on the same frozen version.
2. **Changes are explicit.** Suggestions never overwrite the last approved version.
3. **Structured, not legalistic.** Simple title, summary, terms, optional money/dates, participants, discussions.
4. **Clarity over dashboard theater.** Surface 'needs my attention' first.
5. **Arabic is a first-class UI, not an afterthought.** Perfect RTL, localized formatting, clear vocabulary.
6. **Privacy by default.** No public agreement URLs, private file storage, bounded sharing.
7. **Trustworthy claims.** The service logs account-level acknowledgments; it is not certified digital signature or evidence of legal identity.

## Differentiation hypothesis (to validate with real users)
Different from document-focused contract management: **agreement status and changes are the primary interface, not a multi-page contract editor**. A small shop owner and an individual can use identical collaboration flows without complex enterprise setup. This is a hypothesis rather than a claim of market uniqueness.

## Success metrics (product)
- Time to create a useful agreement (target user-tested threshold: under 5 minutes, not guaranteed).
- % invited participants who successfully view and decide without assistance.
- % of amendments with clear old/new comparison and eventual decision.
- Number of agreements that reach agreed status and have at least one documented commitment or amendment.
- Errors/abandonment at invitation and review steps; privacy/security incident rate.
Avoid vanity counts such as mere agreements created.

## Example contexts
1. Custom kitchen: price, materials, delivery date, installation and a later date change.
2. Three-person trip: each person agrees to responsibilities and expense-sharing terms without required money field.
3. Supplier arrangement: quantities, delivery, conditions, a revised timeline and attachment.

## What Etfago is not
- Not a legally certified contract-signing platform.
- Not a guarantee a person or organization is who they claim.
- Not legal advice or a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.
- Not CRM, ERP, project management, invoicing, or a team chat replacement.
- Not a public file host.

## Release goal
An initial production-worthy release for a single VPS: registered users create and jointly review structured agreements, agree to immutable versions, negotiate amendments, record commitments, attach access-controlled files, receive essential email notices, and export readable summaries. See `09` for phased order.
